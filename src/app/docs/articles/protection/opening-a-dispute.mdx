# Opening a Dispute

If an order arrived and something is genuinely wrong with it, a dispute is how you get your money back. Raising one freezes the payment so it cannot reach the vendor while the case is reviewed.

## Try the vendor first

Most problems are not disputes. A missing accessory, a wrong size, a late delivery — vendors usually fix these the same day if you message them. A dispute is slower than a vendor who simply wants to sort it out.

Raise a dispute when the vendor cannot or will not resolve it, or when what happened was serious enough that you want it on record.

## When you can raise one

You can open a dispute on an order that is marked **Delivered** and has not yet settled.

That means there is a window, and it is not open forever:

<Note type="warning">
  Once an order settles and the vendor has been paid, the dispute route closes.
  Since delivery is confirmed automatically **3 days** after an item goes out for
  delivery, waiting is the main reason buyers lose the ability to dispute. If
  something is wrong, act now.
</Note>

You cannot open a dispute if:

- The order has not been delivered yet — message the vendor or contact [support](/support) instead
- The order has already settled
- A dispute is already open on that part of the order
- That part of the order has already been refunded

## How to raise one

Open the order under **Orders**, find the vendor's part of it, and choose **Raise Dispute**. You will be asked for two things:

### 1. A clear reason

Explain what is wrong in your own words — at least a couple of sentences. Say what you ordered, what you received, and what the difference is.

<Tip>
  Write it for someone who was not there and has never seen the product. "The
  listing said 64GB, the box and the phone settings both show 16GB" decides a
  case. "Fake product" does not.
</Tip>

### 2. Photo evidence

At least one photo is required. Good evidence is specific:

- The item as it arrived, including the packaging
- A close-up of the defect, damage or difference
- The label, model number, or size tag where it contradicts the listing
- The screen showing a fault, if it is a device

## What happens next

1. **The money is frozen.** The vendor cannot be paid while the dispute is open.
2. **The Soraxi team reviews it.** We aim to reach a decision within **5 business days**.
3. **You are told the outcome by email**, and the dispute page on your order shows the current status at any time.

### Possible outcomes

**Resolved in your favour.** A refund is issued to you.

**Resolved in the vendor's favour.** After reviewing the evidence, we could not uphold the claim, and the payment is released to the vendor. The dispute page explains the reasoning.

**More evidence needed.** If the evidence does not settle it either way, we will ask you for more. You have **48 hours** to provide it, and you will be emailed when this happens.

<Note type="warning">
  The 48-hour evidence window is strict. If it passes without a response, the
  dispute is decided on what is already on file.
</Note>

**Decided automatically.** If the Soraxi team does not reach a decision within the 5 business days, the dispute is resolved automatically in your favour and you are refunded. Our deadline is our problem, not yours.

## Following your dispute

Open the order and follow the link to the dispute. You will see the current status, everything you submitted, and a timeline of what has happened.

## Disputes made in bad faith

Disputes exist to protect real buyers. Raising one over something you know to be untrue — claiming an item never arrived when it did, for example — is grounds for action against your account.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I dispute part of an order?**
A: Yes. Each vendor's part is disputed separately, and the rest of the order is unaffected.

**Q: Can I cancel a dispute?**
A: Contact [support](/support) if the vendor has resolved things and you want to withdraw it.

**Q: The item never arrived at all — is that a dispute?**
A: If the order shows Delivered but nothing reached you, yes — raise it straight away. If it is not marked Delivered, contact [support](/support).

**Q: How long does the refund take once I win?**
A: See [Refunds & Cancellations](/docs/protection/refunds-and-cancellations).

**Q: Will the vendor see what I wrote?**
A: The vendor is told a dispute was opened and is part of the review. Write it factually.
