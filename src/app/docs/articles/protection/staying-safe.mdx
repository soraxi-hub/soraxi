# Buying Safely on Soraxi

Soraxi is built so that buying from a stranger on campus is safe by default. Almost every case where someone loses money involves stepping outside that protection voluntarily.

Here is what to know.

## The one rule that matters most

<Warning>
  **Pay on Soraxi. Always.** A payment made by direct bank transfer, in cash on
  the side, or through any link sent to you in a message is a payment with no
  escrow, no dispute process and no refund route. We cannot recover it for you.
</Warning>

It rarely looks like a scam when it happens. It arrives as a favour:

- "Pay me directly and I will take something off the price"
- "The app holds my money for days, just send it to this number"
- "My store is having issues, use this link instead"

All of these mean the same thing: *give up your protection*. A genuine vendor has no reason to ask, because escrow pays them as soon as you have your item.

## Protect your delivery code

Your 6-digit delivery code is proof that you received your order. Anyone who has it can confirm a delivery that never happened.

- Give it **only** when the item is physically in your hands
- Never send it in chat, or ahead of the delivery
- Never give it to "verify your account" or "confirm your order"

See [Your Delivery Code](/docs/buying/delivery-code).

## What Soraxi will never ask you for

<Note type="error">
  No one from Soraxi will ever ask you for your **password**, your **delivery
  code**, your **card details**, or a **bank transfer to an individual account**.
  Anyone who does is not from Soraxi, whatever the message looks like.
</Note>

Be sceptical of any message — in chat, by email, or by SMS — that creates urgency: an account about to be closed, an order about to be cancelled, a refund that needs your card number to process.

## Before you buy

- **Look at the vendor.** Open their storefront: their rating, their reviews, and what else they sell.
- **Read the actual listing**, not just the picture. Check size, model, condition and what is included.
- **Ask before buying** if anything is unclear. A vendor who will not answer a straight question before payment will not answer one after.

## When your order arrives

- **Check the item before releasing your delivery code.** A few seconds is enough to catch the wrong item or obvious damage.
- **Do not confirm delivery for something you have not received**, whatever reason you are given.
- **If something is wrong, act promptly.** Delivery is confirmed automatically 3 days after an item goes out for delivery — see [Confirming Delivery](/docs/buying/confirming-delivery).

## Reporting a problem

**A conversation** — if a vendor is abusive, tries to scam you, pushes you to pay off-platform, or spams you, report the conversation from within it. You will be asked what is wrong, and it goes to the Soraxi team.

**An order** — if an item never arrived or is not what was described, raise a dispute. See [Opening a Dispute](/docs/protection/opening-a-dispute).

**Anything else** — contact [support](/support).

<Note type="info">
  Report off-platform payment requests even when nothing went wrong and even if
  you declined. It is the clearest signal we have about a vendor, and it protects
  the next buyer.
</Note>

## Protecting your account

- Use a password you do not use anywhere else
- Never share it — not with a vendor, not with support, not with a friend
- Sign out on shared or borrowed devices
- If you think someone has access to your account, change your password immediately and contact [support](/support)

See [Account Security](/docs/account/account-security).

## FAQs

**Q: A vendor asked for my number for the delivery. Is that a red flag?**
A: Usually not — riders call. Your phone number is fine to share for a delivery. Your delivery code, password and card details never are.

**Q: I already paid a vendor outside Soraxi. What now?**
A: Contact [support](/support) and report the conversation. We will act on the vendor, but be aware we have no ability to recover money that never entered the platform.

**Q: Is it safe to buy from a brand new vendor?**
A: Every vendor is reviewed before their store goes live. A new vendor has fewer reviews, not less oversight — and escrow protects you either way.

**Q: Someone messaged me claiming to be Soraxi support.**
A: Do not act on it. Contact [support](/support) yourself, through the site.
