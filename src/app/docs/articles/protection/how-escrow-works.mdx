# How Escrow Protects You

When you pay for something on Soraxi, the money does not go to the vendor. Soraxi holds it, and only releases it once your item has actually reached you.

That single mechanism is what makes it safe to buy from someone you have never met.

## What happens to your money

<Note type="success">
  **You pay → Soraxi holds the money → your item arrives → the vendor gets
  paid.** The vendor is never holding both your money and your item.
</Note>

1. **You pay at checkout.** The payment is confirmed and your order is created.
2. **The money is held.** The vendor can see that the order is paid — which is what gives them the confidence to send your item — but they cannot touch the money.
3. **The vendor sends your item.** You get a delivery code when it ships.
4. **Delivery is confirmed.** Either you confirm in the app, or your code is entered when the item is handed to you.
5. **Only then is the vendor paid.**

## When the vendor gets paid

Payment is released when delivery is confirmed. That happens in one of three ways:

- **You confirm in the app** — released immediately
- **Your delivery code is entered** at the doorstep — released immediately
- **Automatically, 3 days** after the item goes out for delivery, if neither of the above happened

<Note type="warning">
  That 3-day automatic release is the part to pay attention to. If your item
  never arrived and you do nothing, the vendor is paid when the 3 days are up.
  Speak up before then — see [Confirming
  Delivery](/docs/buying/confirming-delivery).
</Note>

## What happens if you raise a dispute

Opening a dispute **freezes** the money for that part of your order. It cannot be paid out to the vendor while the dispute is open, and it stays frozen until the Soraxi team reaches a decision.

This is why raising a dispute promptly matters so much: it is what stops the money moving.

See [Opening a Dispute](/docs/protection/opening-a-dispute).

## Orders with more than one vendor

If your order contains items from two vendors, each vendor's part is held, delivered and released **separately**. Confirming one part does not pay the other. A dispute on one part does not affect the other.

## What escrow does and does not cover

**Escrow covers** anything you bought and paid for through Soraxi checkout:

- The item never arrives
- The item is not what was described
- The vendor cancels

**Escrow does not cover** anything paid for outside the platform:

<Warning>
  If you pay a vendor directly — bank transfer, cash, or any link sent to you in
  chat — that money never enters escrow. There is no hold, no dispute process
  and no refund route, no matter what was agreed. See [Buying Safely on
  Soraxi](/docs/protection/staying-safe).
</Warning>

## Why vendors accept this

It is worth understanding, because it explains why the good vendors do not push back on it. Escrow means a vendor can sell to a stranger without worrying about being paid, and confirmed delivery gives them a clean record that they delivered. It protects them from a buyer who takes an item and then claims it never came — the mirror image of the protection it gives you.

The vendors who dislike escrow are, overwhelmingly, the ones you should not buy from.

## FAQs

**Q: Is my money sitting with the vendor while I wait?**
A: No. It is held by Soraxi and is not available to the vendor until delivery is confirmed.

**Q: How quickly do I get money back if something goes wrong?**
A: A refund is issued once the outcome is decided — a vendor cancellation is immediate; a dispute takes as long as the review does. See [Refunds & Cancellations](/docs/protection/refunds-and-cancellations).

**Q: Can a vendor ask me to confirm early so they get paid sooner?**
A: They can ask. You should not do it. Confirming means you have the item.

**Q: What if I never confirm and never dispute?**
A: The order is confirmed automatically 3 days after it goes out for delivery, and the vendor is paid.
