# Refunds & Cancellations

There are four situations where money comes back to you. Each one behaves slightly differently, so it is worth knowing which one you are in.

## 1. The vendor cancels

A vendor can cancel before they ship — usually because the item turned out to be unavailable.

**What you get back:** everything you paid for that vendor's part of the order, including delivery.

**When:** the refund is started as soon as they cancel. You do not need to do anything.

## 2. Delivery failed

The vendor attempted delivery and it could not be completed.

**What you get back:** the amount for the items.

**When:** the refund is started when the vendor marks the delivery as failed.

## 3. You won a dispute

You raised a dispute and it was upheld in your favour.

**What you get back:** a refund of the disputed part of your order.

**When:** once the dispute is decided. See [Opening a Dispute](/docs/protection/opening-a-dispute).

## 4. Your dispute was decided automatically

If the Soraxi team did not reach a decision within the 5 business days we allow ourselves, the dispute resolves automatically in your favour.

**What you get back:** the same as a dispute you won.

## Can I cancel an order myself?

Not from the app. If you want to cancel:

1. **Message the vendor immediately.** Before they ship, cancelling is straightforward for them and they will usually agree.
2. **If they have already shipped**, wait for the item and then decide. If it arrives and is fine but you no longer want it, that is between you and the vendor — a change of mind is not a dispute.
3. **If it never arrives or arrives wrong**, raise a dispute.

<Note type="info">
  The earlier you speak up, the more options exist. Before shipping, almost
  anything can be sorted. After delivery is confirmed and the order settles, very
  little can.
</Note>

## How long a refund takes

Once a refund is issued, the money is returned to the account you paid from. How quickly it lands depends on your bank — typically a few business days.

<Note type="warning">
  A refund goes back to the original payment method. It cannot be redirected to a
  different account, and it cannot be paid out as cash by the vendor.
</Note>

If a refund has been confirmed on your order and nothing has reached your account after several business days, contact [support](/support) with your order reference.

## Returning the item

Soraxi operates a campus delivery model, and returns are arranged between you and the vendor. If a dispute is upheld and the vendor wants the item back, the Soraxi team will tell you what is expected as part of the outcome.

Items that are faulty, damaged or not as described are the cases that matter here. Perishable goods, intimate items and customised products are generally not returnable unless faulty.

## What is not refundable

- **Anything you paid for outside Soraxi.** If you sent money directly to a vendor, it never entered escrow and there is nothing to refund. See [Buying Safely on Soraxi](/docs/protection/staying-safe).
- **A change of mind after a correct delivery.** If you received exactly what was listed and simply do not want it, that is not a dispute — though many vendors will still work with you if you ask.

## FAQs

**Q: I was refunded less than I paid.**
A: Check which situation applies above — a failed delivery refunds the item amount, while a vendor cancellation returns everything including delivery. If the figure still looks wrong, contact [support](/support) with the order reference.

**Q: Can I get a refund as store credit instead?**
A: No. Refunds return to the payment method you used.

**Q: The vendor offered to refund me by transfer instead.**
A: Decline, and report it. A refund handled outside the platform leaves no record and no protection. See [Buying Safely on Soraxi](/docs/protection/staying-safe).

**Q: My order was cancelled but I never got an email.**
A: Check the order under **Orders** for its current status, then contact [support](/support) if it shows as refunded and nothing has arrived.
