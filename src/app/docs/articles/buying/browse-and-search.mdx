# Finding What You Need

Soraxi lists products from vendors on your campus and nearby. There are three ways to find something: search for it, browse a category, or post a request and let vendors come to you.

## Search

The search bar sits at the top of every page. Type what you are looking for and press enter — you will land on the full catalogue, filtered to your search.

Your search stays in the page address, which means you can bookmark a result or send it to a friend and it will open exactly as you left it.

<Tip>
  Search by the plain name of the thing — "phone case", "textbook", "hair
  cream". Vendors write their own listings, so simple words match more products
  than very specific ones.
</Tip>

## Browse by category

Every product sits in a category and a subcategory:

- **Fashion & Accessories** — clothing, footwear, bags, jewelry & watches, headwear
- **Electronics & Gadgets** — phones, phone accessories, power & energy, mini gadgets
- **Books & Stationery** — textbooks, past questions, notebooks & writing materials, study aids
- **Beauty & Personal Care** — skincare, hair products, makeup, perfumes & deodorants, grooming
- **Groceries & Essentials** — dry foodstuff, household items, cooking essentials, quick hostel supplies

Pick a category from the navigation to see everything in it, or open a subcategory to narrow things down.

## Filter and sort

On the catalogue page you can narrow results further:

- **Category** — tick one or more categories to combine them
- **Price** — set the range you are willing to spend
- **Availability** — hide items that are out of stock
- **Sort** — newest first, price low to high, price high to low, or best rated

Your search, sort and page position are kept in the address bar. The remaining filters are refinements and reset when you leave the page.

## Vendor storefronts

Every product page links to the vendor's storefront. Open it to see everything that vendor sells, their rating, and how long they have been on Soraxi. If you have had a good experience with a vendor, their storefront is the fastest way back to them.

## Saving things for later

Tap the heart on any product to add it to your wishlist. See [Wishlist & Reviews](/docs/buying/wishlist-and-reviews) for more.

## Cannot find it?

If nothing on the platform matches what you need, post a request describing it. Vendors who have it can respond to you directly. See [Requests: Ask For What You Need](/docs/buying/requests).

## FAQs

**Q: Why do I see products from vendors far from me?**
A: The catalogue currently shows all live vendors. Check the vendor's storefront and shipping options before ordering to be sure they deliver to you.

**Q: A product I saw yesterday is gone.**
A: Vendors can hide or remove listings at any time, and stock sells out. If you saved it to your wishlist, the item will show as unavailable rather than disappearing.

**Q: Are all these vendors checked?**
A: Yes. Every vendor applies and is reviewed before their store goes live. See [Buying Safely on Soraxi](/docs/protection/staying-safe).
