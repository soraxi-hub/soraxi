# Requests: Ask For What You Need

Sometimes the thing you want is not listed. Rather than checking back every day, post a **request** describing it — vendors browse requests and respond when they can supply what you need.

Requests live at [/requests](/requests). Anyone can read them; you need an account to post one.

## Posting a request

Open **Requests** and choose **Post what you need**. You will be asked for:

- **Title** (required) — what you are looking for, in a few words. Between 3 and 120 characters.
- **Description** (optional) — the detail that matters: size, colour, condition, model, how soon you need it. Up to 1,000 characters.
- **Category** (optional) — helps the right vendors find your request
- **Budget range** (optional) — a minimum and maximum you are willing to spend
- **Images** (optional) — a photo or screenshot of the kind of thing you mean

<Tip>
  A budget range gets far more responses than none. Vendors skip requests they
  cannot price, and a range saves everyone a round of messages.
</Tip>

## What makes a good request

- **Be specific about the essentials.** "Textbook" is hard to fill. "Introduction to Economics, Begg, 12th edition, used is fine" is easy.
- **Say when you need it.** "Before Friday" changes who responds.
- **Add a picture when words are hard.** Especially for fashion, phone parts and anything where a model number is not obvious.

## Managing your requests

Your own requests live under **My Requests**. From there you can:

- **Edit** a request to correct or add detail
- **Mark it fulfilled** once you have found what you needed
- **Delete** it if you no longer need it

### Request statuses

- **Open** — active and visible to vendors
- **Fulfilled** — you found what you were looking for, and it is closed
- **Expired** — no longer active

<Note type="info">
  Mark a request fulfilled once you are sorted. It stops vendors spending time
  on something already solved, and they are more likely to help you next time.
</Note>

## When a vendor responds

A vendor who can supply your request will contact you through Soraxi messaging. From there:

1. Agree what exactly you are buying and for how much
2. Ask the vendor to **list the item** so you can buy it through Soraxi
3. Order it normally, so escrow and the dispute process cover you

<Warning>
  A request is a way to find a vendor, not a way to buy. If someone answers your
  request by asking you to pay them directly — transfer, cash, or a link outside
  Soraxi — that purchase has no escrow and no dispute route. Buy through the
  platform. See [Buying Safely on Soraxi](/docs/protection/staying-safe).
</Warning>

## FAQs

**Q: Who can see my request?**
A: Anyone browsing the requests page. It shows what you wrote, not your address or contact details.

**Q: How long until someone responds?**
A: It depends on what you asked for and how many vendors carry it. Specific, well-priced requests tend to get answers fastest.

**Q: Can I post more than one request?**
A: Yes. Keep them separate — one request per thing you need.

**Q: Nobody responded.**
A: Try widening the budget, adding a picture, or making the title more searchable. Common items are also worth searching for directly; see [Finding What You Need](/docs/buying/browse-and-search).
