# Confirming Delivery

Confirming delivery is what tells Soraxi to release your payment to the vendor. Until it happens, your money stays in escrow.

## The three ways it happens

### 1. You confirm in the app

Open the order under **Orders** and choose **Confirm Delivery**. This is the clearest record of all — it comes from your own account.

### 2. Your delivery code is entered at the doorstep

You give the 6-digit code to whoever hands you the item, and delivery is confirmed on the spot. Nothing else is needed from you afterwards. See [Your Delivery Code](/docs/buying/delivery-code).

### 3. Soraxi confirms it automatically

If neither of the above happens, delivery is confirmed for you **3 days** after the item goes out for delivery, and the vendor is paid.

<Note type="warning">
  Automatic confirmation is the one to watch. If your item never arrived and you
  do nothing, the vendor is paid after 3 days and your options narrow sharply.
</Note>

## Why you should confirm promptly

Confirming quickly is good for you and good for the vendor:

- **For the vendor**, it is the difference between being paid today and being paid in three days. Vendors remember buyers who confirm.
- **For you**, it closes the order cleanly and leaves an unambiguous record of what happened.

## What to do if your item has not arrived

Do **not** wait for the 3 days to run out.

1. **Message the vendor first.** Most late deliveries are ordinary — a rider running behind, a mix-up over the hostel. Vendors usually fix it the same day.
2. **If there is no response or no delivery**, raise a dispute as soon as the order shows Delivered. See [Opening a Dispute](/docs/protection/opening-a-dispute).
3. **If the order is still not marked Delivered** and the vendor has gone quiet, contact [support](/support) with your order reference.

<Warning>
  Never confirm delivery for an item you have not received — not because the
  vendor asks, not to be helpful, not to "release it early". Confirming is
  final: it pays the vendor and removes your strongest protection.
</Warning>

## After confirmation

Once delivery is confirmed:

- The order shows **Delivered**
- Payment is released to the vendor from escrow
- You can leave a review on what you bought — see [Wishlist & Reviews](/docs/buying/wishlist-and-reviews)

You can still raise a dispute after delivery is confirmed, but only for a short period while the order is settling. Once it has fully settled, the dispute route is closed and you would need to contact [support](/support) directly.

## FAQs

**Q: I confirmed by mistake.**
A: Contact [support](/support) immediately with your order reference. The sooner you do, the more can be done.

**Q: The vendor is pressuring me to confirm before delivery.**
A: Do not. Report the conversation — see [Buying Safely on Soraxi](/docs/protection/staying-safe).

**Q: Part of my order arrived and part did not.**
A: Confirm the part you received and deal with the other part separately. Each vendor's portion is confirmed on its own.

**Q: I was away and the 3 days passed.**
A: Contact [support](/support) with your order reference and anything showing the item never arrived.
