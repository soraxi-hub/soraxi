# Cart & Checkout

This guide covers everything between finding a product and paying for it.

## Your cart

Add items from any product page. Your cart is saved to your account, so it follows you between your phone and your laptop.

If you add items before signing in, they are kept and merged into your account cart when you sign in.

### Items from several vendors

You can put products from different vendors in one cart and pay once. Soraxi then splits the order automatically — each vendor sees and fulfils only their own part.

<Note type="info">
  Because each vendor fulfils separately, parts of one order can arrive at
  different times, and each part is tracked and confirmed on its own.
</Note>

## Checkout

Open your cart and choose **Checkout**. You will be asked to:

1. **Confirm your delivery address** — taken from your profile; you can edit it here
2. **Choose a delivery method for each vendor** — vendors set their own options and prices, and the estimated days are shown next to each
3. **Apply a coupon**, if you have one
4. **Review the totals** — items, delivery, any discount, and the final amount
5. **Pay**

<Warning>
  Check your delivery address before paying. Vendors deliver to the address on
  the order, and changing it after payment means contacting the vendor directly
  — which they may not be able to accommodate once the item has shipped.
</Warning>

### Coupons

If you have a coupon code, enter it at checkout and apply it before paying. The discount is applied to your order total and shown in the summary. Coupons can have conditions — a minimum spend, an expiry date, or a limited number of uses — and you will see a message if the code cannot be used on your order.

## Paying

Payment is handled by a secure payment partner. You can pay by card or bank transfer; the options appear when you reach the payment step. Soraxi never sees or stores your card details.

<Note type="success">
  Your money does not go straight to the vendor. It is held by Soraxi until you
  have your item — see [How Escrow Protects
  You](/docs/protection/how-escrow-works).
</Note>

### After you pay

You are returned to Soraxi and shown the result of the payment. Once payment is confirmed:

- Your order appears under **Orders**
- Each vendor in the order is notified and begins processing
- You receive an order confirmation email

### If payment does not complete

Sometimes a payment is interrupted — you close the tab, the network drops, or the transfer is never completed. When that happens:

- The order stays unpaid and no vendor is asked to send anything
- Nothing is taken from your account for an unpaid order
- The unpaid order is closed automatically after a while, and your items stay in your cart so you can try again

If money left your account but your order still shows as unpaid after a short while, contact [support](/support) with the order reference. Do not pay a second time.

<Warning>
  Never pay a vendor directly — by transfer, cash on the side, or any link sent
  in chat. A payment made outside Soraxi has no escrow, no dispute process and
  no refund. See [Buying Safely on Soraxi](/docs/protection/staying-safe).
</Warning>

## FAQs

**Q: Can I change my order after paying?**
A: Not from the app. Message the vendor as soon as possible — before they ship, they may be able to help. Otherwise you can cancel or dispute depending on the stage; see [Refunds & Cancellations](/docs/protection/refunds-and-cancellations).

**Q: Why is delivery charged per vendor?**
A: Each vendor delivers their own items, so each sets their own delivery price. Ordering several items from the same vendor usually means paying delivery once.

**Q: Do I need to be verified to buy?**
A: You need an account. See [Account Verification](/docs/account/verify-account) for what verification unlocks.
