# Wishlist & Reviews

## Your wishlist

The wishlist is where you keep things you are not ready to buy yet.

### Saving an item

Tap the heart on any product — in the catalogue, on a storefront, or on the product page itself. Open **Wishlist** from your account menu to see everything you have saved.

### Managing saved items

- Tap the heart again, or use **Remove**, to take something off the list
- Move an item straight to your cart when you are ready to buy
- Items stay saved until you remove them

<Tip>
  Saving an item does not reserve it or hold the price. Popular items sell out,
  and vendors can change prices at any time.
</Tip>

### When a saved item is no longer available

If a vendor removes a listing or their store stops trading, the item shows as unavailable rather than vanishing, so you know what happened to something you were watching.

## Reviews

Reviews are how good vendors become findable. A vendor's rating comes entirely from buyers like you.

### Leaving a review

Once your order is delivered, open it under **Orders** and choose to review the product. You give:

- **A rating** from 1 to 5 stars
- **A short write-up** describing your experience

Your review appears on the product page, and the product's average rating updates to include it.

### One review per product

You can leave **one review per product**. If you have already reviewed something, you will be told when you try again.

### What makes a review useful

<Tip>
  The most useful reviews say what you expected, what you got, and how the
  vendor handled it. "Arrived next morning, exactly as pictured, seller answered
  my messages" tells the next buyer far more than "good".
</Tip>

- Describe the item's real condition and whether it matched the listing
- Mention delivery speed and how communication went
- Be fair — one slow delivery in an otherwise good experience is worth saying, but so is the good part

### Reviews are not the place for a problem

If something is genuinely wrong — the item never came, or it is not what was described — a review does not get your money back. Raise a dispute instead; see [Opening a Dispute](/docs/protection/opening-a-dispute). Review afterwards, once you know how the vendor handled it.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I edit or delete my review?**
A: Not from the app. Contact [support](/support) if a review needs correcting.

**Q: Can a vendor remove a bad review?**
A: No. Vendors cannot delete or edit reviews left on their products.

**Q: Why can I not review something I did not buy?**
A: Reviews are tied to real orders. That is what makes them worth reading.

**Q: Does my wishlist tell the vendor anything?**
A: No. Your wishlist is private to you.
