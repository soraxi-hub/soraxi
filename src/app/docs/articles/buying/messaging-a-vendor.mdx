# Messaging a Vendor

Messaging lets you ask a vendor about something before you buy, and stay in touch about an order after you have.

Open **Messages** from your account menu to see all your conversations.

## Starting a conversation

### About a product

On any product page, choose the option to message the vendor. This opens a conversation attached to that product, so the vendor can see exactly what you are asking about without you having to explain.

If you have already asked that vendor about that product, you are taken back to the same conversation rather than starting a new one.

### About an order

Open the order under **Orders** and message the vendor from there. The conversation is attached to the order, which means the vendor can see which order you mean.

<Tip>
  Message from the order rather than starting a fresh conversation. A vendor
  handling a dozen deliveries can answer a question far faster when they can see
  which order it belongs to.
</Tip>

## What to ask before buying

The questions worth asking are the ones the listing cannot answer:

- Is this exact size/colour/model in stock right now?
- How soon can you deliver to my hostel?
- Is it new or used, and what condition is it really in?
- Does it come with the accessories in the picture?

## Notices in your conversation

Some messages are not from the vendor — they are notices from Soraxi, shown in the middle of the conversation rather than as a message bubble. These mark things like an order status change or a dispute being opened, so both you and the vendor share the same record of what happened and when.

## Keeping it on Soraxi

<Warning>
  Agree what you like in chat, but **buy on Soraxi**. A vendor who asks you to
  pay by direct transfer, outside the platform, is asking you to give up escrow,
  the dispute process and any route to a refund. There is no version of that
  which is safer or cheaper for you.
</Warning>

This is the single most common way buyers lose money on any marketplace. It usually arrives as a small favour: a discount for paying directly, "the app takes too long", a number to send money to. Once you have paid outside Soraxi, we cannot help you recover it.

## Reporting a conversation

If a vendor is abusive, tries to scam you, pushes you to pay off-platform, or spams you, report the conversation from the conversation itself. You will be asked what is wrong — harassment, a scam, off-platform payment, spam, or something else — and the conversation goes to the Soraxi team for review.

<Note type="info">
  Report the conversation rather than only leaving a bad review. A review warns
  other buyers; a report is what actually gets acted on.
</Note>

## Conversations that are locked

A conversation can be closed to new messages — for example after a dispute has been decided, or if a store is no longer trading. You can still read everything that was said; the record does not disappear.

## FAQs

**Q: How fast will a vendor reply?**
A: Vendors are students and small businesses, not a call centre. Most reply within a day. If an order is affected and you have heard nothing, contact [support](/support).

**Q: Can I message a vendor before making an account?**
A: No — you need to be signed in, so the vendor knows who they are talking to and you keep a record of the conversation.

**Q: Should I share my phone number?**
A: For a delivery it is often reasonable — riders call. Never share your **delivery code**, your password, or any payment details. See [Your Delivery Code](/docs/buying/delivery-code).

**Q: The vendor stopped replying mid-order.**
A: If the item has not arrived, do not wait for the confirmation window to run out. See [Confirming Delivery](/docs/buying/confirming-delivery).
