# Fulfilling an Order

When a buyer pays, you are notified and the order appears under **Orders** in your store. This guide covers what to do with it.

## First: the money is already secured

The buyer has paid, and Soraxi is holding the money. You can pack and send with confidence — you are not waiting to find out whether payment clears.

You are paid once delivery is confirmed. See [How You Get Paid](/docs/payouts/how-you-get-paid).

## Your part of the order

A buyer can order from several vendors at once. You see **only your part** — your items, and the buyer's delivery details. You fulfil and update your part independently of anyone else's.

## Updating the status

Keeping the status current is not admin work — it is what tells the buyer what is happening, and it is what issues the delivery code at the right moment.

### Order Placed → Processing

Set this when you have seen the order and are preparing it. It tells the buyer a person has picked it up.

### Processing → Shipped

Set this when the item leaves you.

<Note type="success">
  Marking an order **Shipped** issues the buyer's 6-digit delivery code and
  creates the confirmation link you will use at handover. Do not skip this
  status — it is what makes proof of delivery possible.
</Note>

### Shipped → Out for Delivery

Set this when the item is with a rider or with you for the final leg. Useful on a longer delivery; you can skip it on a short campus run and go straight to Delivered.

### → Delivered

Set this when the buyer has the item. Confirm it properly — see [Proof of Delivery](/docs/fulfilment/proof-of-delivery).

## The order the statuses must follow

Orders move forward only:

- **Order Placed** → Processing, or Canceled
- **Processing** → Shipped, or Canceled
- **Shipped** → Out for Delivery, Delivered, or Returned
- **Out for Delivery** → Delivered, or Failed Delivery
- **Delivered**, **Canceled**, **Returned**, **Failed Delivery** — final

<Note type="warning">
  You cannot go backwards, and you cannot cancel after shipping. Set each status
  when it is actually true — marking something Shipped before it leaves means
  the buyer starts expecting it, and marking Delivered before it arrives is a
  serious breach.
</Note>

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel while the order is **Order Placed** or **Processing** — before it ships. The buyer is refunded in full, including delivery.

Cancel when you genuinely cannot supply the item. Message the buyer first and explain; a buyer who is told why usually orders again.

<Warning>
  Cancellations are visible in your record and repeated ones damage your
  standing. The cure is not cancelling less — it is keeping stock counts honest
  so the orders never arrive in the first place.
</Warning>

## Failed delivery

If you attempted delivery and it could not be completed — the buyer was unreachable, or the address was wrong — mark it **Failed Delivery**. The buyer is refunded for the items.

Try to reach the buyer before doing this. Most failed deliveries are a missed phone call, and a message through Soraxi will often solve it the same day.

## Handling buyer messages

Buyers can message you about a product or about an order. Open **Messages** in your store menu; conversations are grouped so you can see which order one refers to.

- **Answer quickly.** A buyer who gets a reply almost never escalates.
- **Answer honestly about timing.** "Tomorrow morning" that happens beats "within an hour" that does not.
- **Keep it on Soraxi.** Never ask a buyer to pay you directly — it strips their protection and is grounds for suspension. See [Store Status & Suspension](/docs/storefront/store-status).

## A working routine

1. Check **Orders** at the start of the day
2. Move new orders to **Processing** as you pack them
3. Mark **Shipped** as they leave, and note the delivery code arrangement
4. Confirm delivery with the buyer's code at handover
5. Clear your **Messages** before you close up

## FAQs

**Q: How quickly should I ship?**
A: Within the timeframe your shipping method promises. If you advertise same-day, the order needs to move that day.

**Q: The buyer wants to change their order.**
A: Before you ship, help if you can. After shipping, it is a return conversation.

**Q: The buyer is not answering and I cannot deliver.**
A: Message them through Soraxi so there is a record, try again, and only then mark the delivery failed.

**Q: I marked the wrong status.**
A: Statuses only move forward. Contact [support](/support) with the order reference if you have set something incorrectly.

**Q: Can I deliver myself instead of using a rider?**
A: Yes. Confirm delivery the same way either way — see [Proof of Delivery](/docs/fulfilment/proof-of-delivery).
