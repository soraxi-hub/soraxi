# When a Buyer Disputes an Order

A buyer can dispute a delivered order if they believe something went wrong. This page explains what that means for you.

## What happens immediately

When a dispute is opened on one of your orders:

- **The money for that order is frozen.** It cannot be paid out to you while the dispute is open.
- **You are notified**, and the dispute appears under **Disputes** in your store.
- **The rest of your orders are unaffected.** Only the disputed order is held.

<Note type="info">
  A frozen amount is not a penalty. It is the disputed money being held still
  while the case is looked at — if the dispute is not upheld, it is released to
  you.
</Note>

## How the review works

The Soraxi team reviews the buyer's stated reason and their evidence, alongside the order record — including how delivery was confirmed.

We aim to reach a decision within **5 business days**.

If the evidence does not settle it either way, the buyer is asked for more and has **48 hours** to provide it. While that runs, your funds stay frozen and there is nothing you need to do.

## The outcomes

### Dispute rejected

We could not uphold the buyer's claim. Your frozen funds are released to you and the order settles normally.

### Dispute upheld

The evidence supports the buyer. They are refunded, and a penalty is applied to your account in line with platform policy.

### Resolved automatically

If our team does not decide within the 5 business days we allow ourselves, the dispute resolves in the buyer's favour and the buyer is refunded. Our missed deadline is not treated as your fault, and no penalty applies.

## Penalties and your balance

An upheld dispute carries a penalty. If penalties exceed what is in your wallet, your account carries an outstanding balance, and that balance can restrict withdrawals until it is cleared.

<Note type="warning">
  This is why proof of delivery matters so much. A confirmed delivery code is the
  strongest thing standing between you and an upheld "it never arrived" claim.
  See [Proof of Delivery](/docs/fulfilment/proof-of-delivery).
</Note>

## Following a dispute

Open **Disputes** in your store menu to see the current status, the amount frozen, and a timeline of what has happened. Statuses you will see:

- **Under Review** — being looked at; funds frozen
- **Awaiting Customer Evidence** — more evidence requested from the buyer; nothing needed from you
- **Resolved** — decided by the team
- **Auto-Resolved** — decided automatically after the deadline

## Avoiding disputes in the first place

Nearly every dispute traces back to one of four things:

1. **The listing did not match the item.** Describe condition, contents and limitations accurately. A description that oversells is a dispute waiting to happen.
2. **Delivery was not properly confirmed.** Get the code entered at handover, every time.
3. **The buyer was ignored.** A buyer who cannot get an answer escalates. A buyer who gets a reply usually does not.
4. **Something was sent that should not have been.** Check the item before it leaves you.

<Tip>
  If a buyer contacts you unhappy, treat it as your last cheap opportunity.
  Replacing an item or refunding voluntarily costs you far less than an upheld
  dispute — no penalty, no frozen funds, and often no bad review.
</Tip>

## If you believe a dispute is unfair

Disputes are decided on evidence, so provide it:

- Your delivery confirmation record
- Your messages with the buyer
- Photos of the item before despatch, if you keep them

Contact [support](/support) with the dispute reference and anything relevant. Keeping a photo of anything valuable before it leaves you is a habit worth building.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I contact the buyer during a dispute?**
A: Your existing conversation may be closed to new messages while the case is reviewed. Send what you have to [support](/support) instead.

**Q: How long are my funds frozen?**
A: Until the dispute is decided — normally within 5 business days.

**Q: Does one dispute damage my store?**
A: No. Disputes are a normal part of selling. A pattern of upheld ones is what causes trouble — see [Store Status & Suspension](/docs/storefront/store-status).

**Q: The buyer disputed but I have a confirmed delivery code.**
A: Say so, and point to the order. A confirmed code is strong evidence and it is on the record.

**Q: Can I refund a buyer directly to end a dispute?**
A: No. Never send money to a buyer outside Soraxi — you would have no record of it, and the dispute would still be decided without it.
