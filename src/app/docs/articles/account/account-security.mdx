# Account Security

Your Soraxi account holds your order history, your delivery address and — if you sell — your earnings. This page covers how to keep it yours.

## Password requirements

Every Soraxi password must have:

- At least **8 characters**
- At least one **uppercase** letter (A–Z)
- At least one **lowercase** letter (a–z)
- At least one **number** (0–9)
- At least one **special character** (!@#$%^&\*)

The sign-up form shows your password strength as you type — weak, moderate or strong. Aim for strong.

<Tip>
  Three unrelated words plus a number and a symbol beats a short scramble you
  cannot remember: `Lagos-Kettle7Moon!` is both stronger and easier to recall
  than `P@ss1234`.
</Tip>

## Choosing a good password

- **Do not reuse a password** from another site. If that site is breached, your Soraxi account goes with it.
- **Do not use anything guessable** — your name, matric number, phone number, or date of birth.
- **Do not let a shared browser remember it** on a device other people use.

## Changing your password

1. Open **Security** from your account menu
2. Choose **Change Password**
3. Enter your current password, then your new one twice
4. Save

You will need your current password to make the change — which is what stops someone who finds your unlocked phone from locking you out.

Change it immediately if:

- You think someone else knows it
- You used it on another site that was breached
- You signed in on a device you do not control and could not sign out

## Forgot your password?

Use the **Forgot Password** link on the sign-in page. See [Resetting Your Password](/docs/account/password-reset).

## Signing in on shared devices

Campus life means borrowed laptops and cyber cafés. If you sign in on a device that is not yours:

- Use a private or incognito window
- Sign out when you are finished — closing the tab is not the same thing
- Never save the password to that browser

## What Soraxi will never ask for

<Note type="error">
  No one from Soraxi will ever ask for your **password**, your **delivery code**,
  or your **card details** — not by email, not in chat, not over the phone.
</Note>

If a message asks for any of these, it is not from us, however official it looks. Contact [support](/support) directly through the site if you are unsure.

## Keeping your account details current

Your profile holds the address your orders are delivered to and the email your account notices go to. Keep both accurate:

- Open **Edit Profile** from your account menu to update your details
- A wrong address means a failed delivery; a dead email means you miss dispute deadlines

## If your account is compromised

Act in this order:

1. **Change your password** immediately, if you still have access
2. **Check your recent orders** for anything you did not place
3. **Contact [support](/support)** and tell us what happened

<Warning>
  If you sell on Soraxi, a compromised account is also a compromised store.
  Change your store password separately — a store account has its own login. See
  [Store Settings](/docs/storefront/store-settings).
</Warning>

## FAQs

**Q: Can I use the same password for my account and my store?**
A: You can, but do not. They are separate logins precisely so that one being exposed does not hand over the other.

**Q: Does Soraxi store my card details?**
A: No. Payments are handled by a secure payment partner and card details never reach Soraxi.

**Q: I am signed in on my old phone and cannot get to it.**
A: Change your password. Contact [support](/support) if you believe the device is in someone else's hands.
