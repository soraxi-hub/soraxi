# Resetting Your Password

If you cannot sign in because you have forgotten your password, you can reset it yourself from the sign-in page.

## Resetting your password

1. Go to the sign-in page and choose **Forgot Password**
2. Enter the **email address on your account**
3. Check your inbox for a reset email from Soraxi
4. Open the link in that email
5. Enter your new password twice and save
6. Sign in with the new password

<Note type="info">
  The reset link works **once** and expires after a while. If you request several
  resets, use the most recent email — older links stop working.
</Note>

## Your new password

The same rules apply as when you signed up:

- At least 8 characters
- An uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character

Do not reuse a password from another site. See [Account Security](/docs/account/account-security).

## Vendors: resetting a store password

Your store has its own login, separate from your personal account. If you have forgotten your **store** password, use the same Forgot Password flow and enter your **store email address** — the address you registered the store with, not your personal one.

<Tip>
  If you are not sure which email a store uses, sign in to your personal account
  first — your stores are listed on your profile.
</Tip>

## If the email does not arrive

Work through these in order:

1. **Wait a couple of minutes.** Email is not instant.
2. **Check spam and promotions.** Reset emails land there more often than anywhere else.
3. **Check you used the right address.** It must be the exact address on the account. If you have more than one email, try the others.
4. **Check for typos** in what you entered.
5. **Try once more.** A fresh request sends a new link.

If nothing arrives after that, contact [support](/support) from an address you control and tell us the account email you are trying to reset.

<Note type="warning">
  For security, the reset page does not tell you whether an email address has an
  account. A confirmation message does not necessarily mean an email is on its
  way to that address.
</Note>

## If you think someone else has your password

Reset it immediately, then:

- Check your recent orders for anything you did not place
- If you sell, check your store for changes you did not make
- Contact [support](/support) if anything looks wrong

## FAQs

**Q: Can I reset my password without access to my email?**
A: No — the reset link is the proof that the account is yours. Contact [support](/support) if you have permanently lost access to the address.

**Q: The link says it is invalid or expired.**
A: Request a fresh one. Links are single-use and time-limited, and an older link stops working once a newer one is issued.

**Q: Can I change my password without forgetting it?**
A: Yes, and it is quicker. See [Account Security](/docs/account/account-security).

**Q: Do I have to reset my store password when I reset my personal one?**
A: No. They are independent — which is exactly why they should not share a password.
