# Fees & Commission

There are two charges on Soraxi, and nothing else. No listing fees, no monthly fee, no charge for opening a store.

1. **A commission on each sale**
2. **A fee on each withdrawal**

## Commission per sale

Commission is **5% of the item value, plus a flat fee that depends on the size of the sale**:

| Sale value        | Commission          |
| ----------------- | ------------------- |
| Under ₦2,500      | 5% + ₦100           |
| ₦2,500 – ₦4,999   | 5% (no flat fee)    |
| ₦5,000 and above  | 5% + ₦200           |

### Worked examples

**A ₦1,000 sale**
5% = ₦50, plus ₦100 flat = **₦150 commission**. You receive **₦850**.

**A ₦3,000 sale**
5% = ₦150, no flat fee = **₦150 commission**. You receive **₦2,850**.

**A ₦10,000 sale**
5% = ₦500, plus ₦200 flat = **₦700 commission**. You receive **₦9,300**.

<Note type="info">
  Notice the middle band. A sale just over ₦2,500 can leave you with **more**
  than one just under it, because the ₦100 flat fee drops away. Worth knowing
  when you are pricing near that line.
</Note>

## What commission is charged on

Commission is calculated on the **item value the buyer pays you**, after any discount has been applied.

**Delivery is not included.** What you charge for delivery is yours in full — no commission is taken from it.

### With a coupon

If a buyer uses a coupon, commission is calculated on the discounted amount, not the original price.

## Withdrawal fee

When you withdraw from your wallet to your bank account:

- **1% of the amount withdrawn**
- **plus ₦50**

**Withdrawing ₦10,000:** 1% = ₦100, plus ₦50 = **₦150 fee**.

### Withdrawal limits

- **Minimum:** ₦1,000 per request
- **Maximum:** ₦100,000 per request

<Tip>
  Because there is a fixed ₦50 on every withdrawal, fewer larger withdrawals cost
  less than many small ones. Withdrawing ₦10,000 once costs ₦150; withdrawing
  ₦1,000 ten times costs ₦600.
</Tip>

## What you are not charged for

- Opening a store
- Listing products, however many
- Products that do not sell
- Buyer messages
- Cancelled orders — no commission is charged on a sale that did not complete

## Penalties

Separately from fees, an upheld dispute can result in a penalty against your account, in line with platform policy. This is not a fee on trading — it only arises when a dispute is decided against you.

See [When a Buyer Disputes an Order](/docs/fulfilment/vendor-disputes).

## Pricing with fees in mind

<Tip>
  Work out your take-home before you set a price, not after your first payout.
  On a ₦1,500 item you keep ₦1,325; on a ₦5,000 item you keep ₦4,550. Price so
  that the sale is worth making after commission.
</Tip>

For low-value items, the flat fee is the part that bites. Bundling two ₦800 items into one ₦1,600 sale means paying the flat fee once rather than twice.

## FAQs

**Q: Where do I see the exact commission on an order?**
A: Each order in your store shows the breakdown — what the buyer paid, the commission, and your settlement amount.

**Q: Is commission taken before or after I am paid?**
A: It is deducted when the order settles. The amount arriving in your wallet is already net of commission.

**Q: Do I pay commission on a refunded order?**
A: A cancelled order is refunded to the buyer in full and you are not charged commission on it.

**Q: Does the commission rate change as I sell more?**
A: The rates above apply to all vendors. Any change would be announced in advance.

**Q: Are these figures inclusive of tax?**
A: These are the platform's charges. Your own tax obligations are yours to manage.
