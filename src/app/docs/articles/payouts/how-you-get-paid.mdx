# How You Get Paid

Money reaches you in three stages. Understanding them explains why your wallet shows what it shows.

**Buyer pays → held in escrow → your wallet → your bank account**

## Stage 1 — The buyer pays

When a buyer checks out, Soraxi collects and holds the money. The vendor never has it yet, and neither does the buyer.

This is what makes it safe for a stranger to order from you: they know their money is protected, which is why they order at all.

<Note type="success">
  The order in your dashboard is a **paid** order. You can pack and send without
  wondering whether payment will clear.
</Note>

## Stage 2 — Delivery is confirmed, and your wallet is credited

Escrow releases when delivery is confirmed. That happens in one of three ways:

- **The buyer's delivery code is entered** at handover — immediate
- **The buyer confirms** in their app — immediate
- **Automatically, 3 days** after the item goes out for delivery, if neither happened

The amount credited is the sale value **minus commission**, plus whatever you charged for delivery. See [Fees & Commission](/docs/payouts/fees-and-commission).

<Tip>
  Getting the delivery code entered pays you today instead of in three days. It
  is the single fastest lever you have on your own cash flow. See [Proof of
  Delivery](/docs/fulfilment/proof-of-delivery).
</Tip>

## Stage 3 — You withdraw to your bank

Once money is available in your wallet, you request a withdrawal to your bank account. See [Withdrawing Your Earnings](/docs/payouts/withdrawals).

## Your wallet balances

Open **Wallet** in your store menu. Amounts sit in one of three states:

### Pending

Orders that have been paid for but not yet confirmed as delivered. The money is yours in prospect, but it is still in escrow and cannot be withdrawn.

**How it moves:** confirm delivery, and it becomes available.

### Available

Money that has cleared and can be withdrawn now.

### Frozen

Money tied to an open dispute. It cannot be withdrawn while the case is being reviewed. If the dispute is not upheld, it is released to you.

See [When a Buyer Disputes an Order](/docs/fulfilment/vendor-disputes).

<Note type="info">
  A large **Pending** balance is normal for a busy store — it is orders in
  flight. If it stays large while deliveries are completing, check that
  deliveries are actually being confirmed.
</Note>

## When money does not arrive

**A cancelled order.** Cancel before shipping and the buyer is refunded in full. Nothing is credited to you, and no commission is charged.

**A failed delivery.** The buyer is refunded for the items.

**An upheld dispute.** The buyer is refunded, and a penalty may apply. If a penalty exceeds your available balance, your account carries an outstanding amount that can restrict withdrawals until it is cleared.

## Getting paid faster

1. **Ship promptly.** Nothing settles until it is delivered.
2. **Always get the delivery code entered.** Immediate release instead of a three-day wait.
3. **Keep listings accurate.** Disputes freeze money; accurate listings prevent disputes.
4. **Set up your bank account before your first sale**, so nothing waits on paperwork.

## FAQs

**Q: How soon after a sale can I have the money in my bank?**
A: Confirm delivery with the code and the amount becomes available immediately; then it depends on how quickly you request a withdrawal and how long your bank takes.

**Q: Why is my wallet lower than the order totals?**
A: Commission is deducted at settlement. Each order shows its breakdown.

**Q: Can I be paid per order automatically?**
A: Withdrawals are requested by you, so you control when money moves.

**Q: A delivered order has not credited my wallet.**
A: Check that it is marked **Delivered** rather than Shipped or Out for Delivery. If it is Delivered and the wallet has not moved, contact [support](/support) with the order reference.

**Q: What happens to my money if my store is suspended?**
A: It remains yours, subject to any open disputes or outstanding balance. Withdrawals may be held during the review.
