# Store Status & Suspension

Your store has a status that determines whether buyers can see and order from you.

## The statuses

### Pending

Your store exists but onboarding is not finished. Buyers cannot see it, and you cannot list products.

**What to do:** complete all three onboarding steps — profile, shipping and terms. See [Setting Up Your Store](/docs/storefront/create-storefront).

### Active

Your store is live. Your storefront is public, approved products appear in search, and you can receive orders.

This is where you want to be, and it happens as soon as onboarding is complete — the vetting already happened when your application was reviewed.

### Suspended

Your store has been taken out of the marketplace pending review.

While suspended:

- Your storefront and products are not visible to buyers
- You cannot receive new orders
- **You are still responsible for orders already placed** — deliver them
- Money already in your wallet stays yours, subject to the outcome of any open cases

### Rejected

The store was not approved to trade. See the reason given and contact [support](/support) if you believe it is wrong.

## What causes a suspension

Suspension is not the first response to an ordinary mistake — a single late delivery or one upheld dispute will not do it. It follows a pattern, or something serious enough on its own:

- **Repeated failure to deliver** — cancelling orders you cannot fulfil, or letting deliveries fail
- **A pattern of upheld disputes** — buyers repeatedly not getting what they paid for
- **Listings that misrepresent goods** — descriptions or photos that do not match what ships
- **Prohibited items** — see [Managing Your Products](/docs/storefront/manage-products)
- **Pushing buyers off-platform** — asking for direct payment, which strips buyers of every protection they have
- **Abusive conduct** toward buyers or staff

<Warning>
  Asking a buyer to pay you directly is the single fastest way to lose a store.
  It removes the buyer's escrow protection and their route to a refund, and it is
  treated as a serious breach rather than a minor one.
</Warning>

## Staying in good standing

The vendors who never think about this page tend to do the same things:

- **Only list what you can actually supply.** Most vendor trouble starts with a listing that could not be fulfilled.
- **Keep stock counts honest.** Hide or zero out what you do not have.
- **Ship when you say you will**, and update the order status as you go.
- **Answer messages.** A buyer who gets a reply rarely escalates; a buyer who is ignored does.
- **Use the delivery code.** It is your proof of delivery if a dispute is ever raised. See [Proof of Delivery](/docs/fulfilment/proof-of-delivery).
- **Deal with problems directly.** Offering a fix before a dispute is opened is cheaper for you in every sense.

## If your store is suspended

1. **Read the notice.** It states the reason.
2. **Fulfil outstanding orders.** Buyers who have paid still expect their items, and failing them makes the outcome worse.
3. **Reply to [support](/support)** with anything relevant — delivery records, messages, evidence.
4. **Fix the underlying cause** before asking for reinstatement.

Suspensions are reviewed. Stores that address the cause are reinstated; stores that repeat it are not.

## Disputes and your standing

An upheld dispute means a buyer is refunded, and it can carry a penalty against your account. Enough unpaid balance can restrict withdrawals until it is cleared.

Disputes are a normal part of selling and one does not mark you out. A pattern does. See [When a Buyer Disputes an Order](/docs/fulfilment/vendor-disputes).

## FAQs

**Q: Will I be told before I am suspended?**
A: For anything short of a serious breach, you will normally hear about the problem first. Serious cases — fraud, off-platform payment demands, prohibited goods — can be immediate.

**Q: What happens to money in my wallet if I am suspended?**
A: It remains yours, subject to any open disputes or outstanding balance. Withdrawals may be held while the review is under way.

**Q: Can I open a new store instead of appealing?**
A: No. Applying again to get around a suspension is grounds for permanent removal.

**Q: My store shows Pending and I have completed onboarding.**
A: Sign out and back in first. If it persists, contact [support](/support).
