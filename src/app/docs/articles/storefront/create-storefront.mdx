# Setting Up Your Store

This guide picks up **after** your vendor application has been approved. If you have not applied yet, start at [Applying to Sell on Soraxi](/docs/storefront/vendor-waitlist).

<Note type="info">
  You do not create a store yourself on Soraxi. When your application is
  approved, we create it for you and email your login details. There is no store
  sign-up form to fill in.
</Note>

## Step 1 — Sign in to your store

Your approval email contains:

- Your **store email** — the address you applied with
- A **temporary password**
- A link to your store dashboard

Sign in with those.

<Warning>
  Change the temporary password as soon as you are in. It was generated for you
  and sent by email, which makes it the weakest password your store will ever
  have. Go to **Security → Change Password** in your store menu.
</Warning>

Your store account is **separate from your personal Soraxi account**. Two different logins, two different passwords — deliberately, so that one being compromised does not expose the other.

## Step 2 — Complete onboarding

Onboarding is three steps. You cannot start selling until all three are done.

### Store profile

- **Store name** — carried over from your application
- **Store description** — what you sell and why someone should buy from you

Your description is what a buyer reads when deciding whether to trust you. Say what you actually stock, where you deliver, and how quickly.

<Tip>
  Write for a student deciding between you and a vendor they already know.
  "Phone accessories and chargers, delivered same day across campus, in stock and
  tested before it leaves me" beats any amount of generic marketing copy.
</Tip>

### Shipping

Set up at least one delivery method. Each one has:

- A **name** buyers see at checkout
- A **price**
- An **estimated delivery time** in days
- A **description** of what the method covers

You can add more later and change them at any time. See [Setting Up Shipping](/docs/storefront/shipping-setup).

### Terms

Read the vendor terms and accept them. The date you accepted is recorded on your store.

## Step 3 — You are live

Once onboarding is complete, your store goes **Active** immediately. There is no second waiting period — the vetting already happened at the application stage.

Being live means:

- Your storefront is visible to buyers
- Your products can appear in search and category pages once approved
- You can receive orders

## Step 4 — Add your first products

An empty store sells nothing. Add products from **Products → Upload**.

Each product goes through a short review before it appears publicly. Full details, including what gets rejected, are in [Managing Your Products](/docs/storefront/manage-products).

<Tip>
  Start with five to ten products you can definitely supply today, rather than a
  large catalogue you cannot fulfil. Your first few orders decide your rating,
  and your rating decides everything after that.
</Tip>

## Step 5 — Set up how you get paid

Add your bank account before your first order arrives, so your earnings are not sitting waiting when you want them.

See [Payout Configuration](/docs/storefront/payout-settings) and [How You Get Paid](/docs/payouts/how-you-get-paid).

## Your setup checklist

- Signed in and **changed the temporary password**
- Store profile written
- At least one shipping method set up
- Vendor terms accepted
- First products uploaded
- Bank account added for payouts

## Common problems

### "I never received my login email"

Check spam and promotions first. If it is genuinely not there, check your application status at [/store/waitlist/status](/store/waitlist/status) with your email and reference ID — it will confirm whether the store was created. If it was and you still have no email, contact [support](/support).

### "I cannot sign in with the temporary password"

Copy and paste it rather than typing it, and check for a trailing space. If it still fails, use **Forgot Password** on the sign-in page with your **store email**. See [Resetting Your Password](/docs/account/password-reset).

### "I am signed in but I cannot add products"

Onboarding is not finished. Complete all three steps — profile, shipping and terms.

### "My store name is wrong"

It comes from your application. You can update your store profile from **Profile** in your store menu. See [Store Settings](/docs/storefront/store-settings).

## FAQs

**Q: Can I run more than one store?**
A: Each approved application creates one store. Apply separately for a genuinely separate business.

**Q: Do I need a registered business or a CAC number?**
A: No. Individual student vendors are welcome. A CAC number strengthens an application but is not required.

**Q: What does it cost?**
A: Nothing to open or run. Soraxi takes a commission per sale — see [Fees & Commission](/docs/payouts/fees-and-commission).

**Q: Can I change my store email?**
A: It is your login, so contact [support](/support) rather than changing it yourself.

**Q: How soon can I take orders?**
A: As soon as onboarding is done and you have at least one approved product.
