# Applying to Sell on Soraxi

Soraxi is an invite-only marketplace for vendors. You cannot open a store directly — you apply, we review the application, and approved vendors get a store.

That is deliberate. Buyers trust Soraxi because every vendor on it was checked by a person first, and that trust is worth more to you as a seller than an open sign-up would be.

## Before you apply

You need:

- **A Soraxi account**, signed in. Your application is attached to it. See [Creating Your Account](/docs/account/create-account).
- **A business name** you want to trade under
- **A working email address** for the business — this becomes your store login, so use one you check
- **A phone number** we can reach you on
- **Your institution**
- **The main category** you sell in
- **1 to 4 photos of products you actually sell**

Optional, but they strengthen an application:

- **A CAC number**, if your business is registered
- **An Instagram handle** or another link showing you already trade

<Tip>
  The product photos carry more weight than anything else in the form. Real
  photographs of real stock, taken by you, tell us more than any description.
  Pictures pulled from Google are obvious and count against you.
</Tip>

## Filling in the application

Start at [/store/waitlist](/store/waitlist). The form has two steps.

### Step 1 — Business & Contact

- **Business name** — what you want your store called
- **Owner name** — your name
- **Email** — the address that becomes your store login
- **Phone number**
- **Institution** — pick yours from the list
- **Category** — the main thing you sell
- **Do you dropship?** — whether you hold stock yourself or source it on order

<Note type="info">
  Answer the dropshipping question honestly. It is not disqualifying, and it
  helps us set expectations about your delivery times. Being caught out later is
  far worse than saying yes now.
</Note>

### Step 2 — Proof & Samples

- **Product sample images** (required) — between 1 and 4 photos
- **CAC number** (optional) — if registered
- **Instagram handle** (optional) — your business account, in any format
- **Other proof** (optional) — a website or other social link

Then submit.

## Your reference ID

When you submit, you get a **reference ID**. Save it.

It is how you check your application later, together with the email address you applied with, at [/store/waitlist/status](/store/waitlist/status).

<Warning>
  Write the reference ID down before you close the page. You need it, with your
  email, to check your status.
</Warning>

## What happens next

Your application goes into a review queue. A person reads it — the business, the category, the samples, and how many vendors we already have in that category at your institution.

### Application statuses

- **Pending** — with us, awaiting review
- **Approved** — accepted; your store has been created and your login details emailed to you
- **Active** — your store is set up and ready to use
- **Rejected** — not accepted this time, with a reason

## If you are approved

You do **not** need to create a store yourself. On approval, Soraxi creates it for you and emails your store login details, including a temporary password.

From there:

1. Sign in to your store with the emailed credentials
2. **Change the temporary password immediately**
3. Complete onboarding — store profile, shipping, and terms

See [Setting Up Your Store](/docs/storefront/create-storefront).

## If you are rejected

The status page shows the reason. It is usually one of:

- Product samples that do not show real stock
- Contact details we could not verify
- A category already well covered at your institution
- Not enough information to make a decision

You are welcome to apply again. Address the reason first — a resubmission that fixes nothing gets the same answer.

## FAQs

**Q: How long does review take?**
A: It depends on the queue. Check your status with your reference ID rather than reapplying — a second application for the same email is rejected as a duplicate.

**Q: Can I apply with the same email as my personal account?**
A: Your store login must be unique across the platform. Using a separate business address keeps the two accounts cleanly apart.

**Q: The business name I want is taken.**
A: Store names are unique. You will be told at application time if it is already in use — choose another.

**Q: Can I sell in more than one category?**
A: Apply with your main category. Once your store is live you can list products across the catalogue.

**Q: Does it cost anything to apply?**
A: No. Applying and opening a store are free. Soraxi earns a commission on sales — see [Fees & Commission](/docs/payouts/fees-and-commission).
