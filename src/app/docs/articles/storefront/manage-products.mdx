# Managing Your Products

Everything about listing what you sell — adding products, getting them approved, and keeping them accurate.

Open **Products** from your store menu.

## The products dashboard

The dashboard lists everything you have listed, with:

- **Counts** of your products by status
- **Search** across your listings
- **Filters** by status and category
- **Actions** on each product — edit, hide, archive

## Adding a product

Go to **Products → Upload**.

### Product details

- **Name** (required) — between 5 and 100 characters
- **Description** (required) — at least 50 characters
- **Specifications** (required) — at least 50 characters
- **Price** (required) — between ₦500 and ₦100,000
- **Quantity** (required) — at least 1
- **Category and subcategory** (required) — pick where the product belongs
- **Target audience** (required)

<Tip>
  Write the description a buyer needs, not the one you would skim. Condition, what
  is included, what is not, and any limitation. Descriptions that answer questions
  up front get fewer messages and fewer disputes.
</Tip>

### Products with sizes

If a product comes in sizes, add each size with its own **price** and **quantity**. Each size is stocked separately, so a size that sells out stops being orderable without affecting the rest.

### Images

- Up to **3 images** per product
- Up to **4MB** each
- **JPG, PNG or WebP**

Your first image is what buyers see in search and category pages. Make it the clearest one.

<Tip>
  Photograph your actual stock in daylight against a plain background. On a
  campus marketplace, a real photo of the real item outperforms a manufacturer
  press shot — buyers are deciding whether to trust *you*, not the brand.
</Tip>

### Writing a description with AI

If you are stuck, the upload form can draft a description from the details you have already entered. Use it as a starting point and edit it — you know your stock, and a description that does not match what arrives is what disputes are made of.

The AI draft is limited to a number of generations per hour per store, so it is there to unblock you rather than to write your whole catalogue.

### Publishing

Publishing requires your **store password**. That is deliberate — it means someone who finds your unlocked phone cannot list items in your name.

You can either:

- **Save as draft** — kept private, nothing is reviewed, finish it later
- **Publish** — submitted for review

## Product statuses

- **Draft** — yours only, not submitted, not visible
- **Pending** — submitted and awaiting review
- **Approved** — live and visible to buyers
- **Rejected** — not approved, with a reason
- **Archived** — taken out of your active catalogue without being deleted

## Review

Every published product is reviewed before it appears publicly. Review checks that the listing is accurate, the images are of the actual product, the category is right, and the item is allowed on the platform.

### What gets rejected

- Images that are not of the product being sold
- Descriptions that contradict the images or the title
- Wrong or deliberately misleading category
- Missing detail a buyer needs to make a decision
- Prohibited items (below)

If a product is rejected you are told why. Fix the reason and resubmit.

## What you may not sell

<Warning>
  Illegal goods, counterfeit or replica products, hazardous materials, stolen
  goods, prescription medication, weapons, and anything you cannot legally sell in
  Nigeria. Listing them gets the product removed and can get your store
  suspended.
</Warning>

If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask [support](/support) before listing it.

## Editing a product

Open a product and choose **Edit**. You can update the name, description, specifications, price, quantity, images and category.

<Note type="warning">
  Substantial edits to a live product send it back for review. Changing price or
  quantity does not — so restocking and repricing are immediate.
</Note>

### Keep stock accurate

The fastest way to damage your rating is to sell something you cannot supply. If you run out, set the quantity to zero or hide the product rather than cancelling orders after the fact — cancellations refund the buyer and count against you.

## Hiding and archiving

- **Hide** — takes a product out of the catalogue temporarily; use it for seasonal or out-of-stock items
- **Archive** — retires a product you no longer sell, without losing its history

Both are reversible.

## Common problems

### "My product is still pending"

Reviews are done by people and take a little time. If it has been sitting far longer than your others, contact [support](/support) with the product name.

### "My images will not upload"

Check the file is JPG, PNG or WebP, under 4MB, and that you are not over the 3-image limit. A photo straight from a modern phone camera is often over 4MB — resize before uploading.

### "Price or quantity changes are not saving"

Make sure the price is between ₦500 and ₦100,000, and the quantity is a whole number of at least 1. For sized products, each size needs its own valid price and quantity.

### "I cannot publish"

Every required field must be filled and the length minimums met — 50 characters for both description and specifications — and you need your store password.

## FAQs

**Q: Can I delete a product?**
A: Archive it. Products are kept so past orders and reviews stay intact.

**Q: How many products can I list?**
A: There is no cap. List what you can actually supply.

**Q: Can I list something I do not have yet?**
A: Only if you can deliver it in your stated timeframe. Listing what you cannot supply leads to cancellations, refunds and a falling rating.

**Q: Do rejected products count against my store?**
A: One rejection is a correction. A pattern of them is a problem — see [Store Status & Suspension](/docs/storefront/store-status).
