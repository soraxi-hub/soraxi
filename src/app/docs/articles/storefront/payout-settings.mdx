# Payout Configuration

Your payout account is the bank account your earnings are transferred to. Set it up before your first sale so nothing is waiting when you want to withdraw.

Open **Payment Setup** from your store menu.

## Before you start

You need:

- Your **bank name**
- Your **10-digit account number**
- An account whose **name matches your store's registered details**

<Warning>
  The account holder name must match the details your store is registered with.
  Transfers to a mismatched account fail, and unwinding a failed transfer takes
  far longer than getting it right the first time.
</Warning>

## Adding a payout account

### Step 1 — Open the form

On the Payment Setup page, choose **Add Account**. Any accounts you have already added are listed above.

### Step 2 — Select your bank

Pick your bank from the list. It covers Nigerian commercial banks and the major digital banks.

<Tip>
  Several banks have similar names. Check you have the exact one — the wrong
  selection is the most common cause of a failed transfer.
</Tip>

### Step 3 — Enter your account number

Enter your **10-digit** account number. No spaces or dashes.

### Step 4 — Confirm the account name

Soraxi looks the account up automatically and shows the account holder name registered with the bank. This takes a few seconds.

**Check the name that comes back carefully.** It is the name your bank has on file, and it is what the transfer will go to.

If the lookup fails:

- Re-check the account number, digit by digit
- Confirm you selected the right bank
- Try again in a moment — bank lookup services are occasionally briefly unavailable
- If it keeps failing, check with your bank that the account is active

### Step 5 — Save

Review everything and save. The account is then available for withdrawals.

## Managing your accounts

You can hold up to **three** payout accounts. Useful if you keep a business account separate from a personal one, or want a backup for when a bank is having problems.

- Set which account withdrawals default to
- Remove an account you no longer use
- Add a replacement before removing an old one, so you are never without a route to withdraw

<Note type="warning">
  Update your payout details **before** the old account closes. A withdrawal to a
  closed account fails and the money returns to your wallet, but only after a
  delay.
</Note>

## What it costs to withdraw

- **Minimum:** ₦1,000 per request
- **Maximum:** ₦100,000 per request
- **Fee:** 1% of the amount, plus ₦50

See [Fees & Commission](/docs/payouts/fees-and-commission) for the full picture, including sales commission.

## How withdrawals work

Adding a payout account does not move any money — you request each withdrawal yourself, when you want it.

A request moves through **Initiated → Processing → Completed**, or **Failed** if the transfer is rejected, in which case the amount returns to your wallet. You are emailed when it completes or fails.

How quickly the money lands once sent depends on your bank.

Full details are in [Withdrawing Your Earnings](/docs/payouts/withdrawals).

## Security

- Your bank details are only ever used to pay you
- Soraxi never asks for your **card details**, your **bank password**, or any **OTP** from your bank
- Only someone signed in to your store can view or change payout details — which is another reason to keep your store password strong and private

<Note type="error">
  No one from Soraxi will ever ask you to "verify" your payout account by sending
  money, sharing a bank OTP, or entering your bank login anywhere. Anyone asking
  is attempting fraud.
</Note>

## Common problems

### "Account verification failed"

Almost always a wrong digit or the wrong bank. Re-check both. If the account is new, very recently opened accounts sometimes take time to appear in bank lookup systems.

### "Account name does not match"

The name returned by your bank differs from your store details. Use an account in the correct name — you cannot withdraw to an account belonging to someone else.

### "I cannot find my bank"

Check for alternative spellings or the bank's former name. If it is genuinely missing, contact [support](/support) with the bank's full name.

### A withdrawal failed

The amount is returned to your wallet. Correct the account details and request again. See [Withdrawing Your Earnings](/docs/payouts/withdrawals).

## FAQs

**Q: Can I withdraw to a friend's or family member's account?**
A: No. The payout account must be in the name your store is registered under.

**Q: Can I change my payout account after requesting a withdrawal?**
A: Yes, but a withdrawal already in progress goes to the account it was requested against.

**Q: Do I need a business account?**
A: No. A personal account in your own name is fine.

**Q: Is my account number visible to buyers?**
A: No. Payout details are visible only to you.
