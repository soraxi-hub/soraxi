# Store Settings

Everything you can change about your store once it is running.

## Store profile

Open **Profile** from your store menu to update:

- **Store description** — what you sell and what buyers can expect
- **Contact details** — how buyers and Soraxi reach you

Your description is the main thing a buyer reads before deciding to order. Revisit it as your stock changes — a description listing things you no longer sell costs you orders and creates disputes.

<Tip>
  Update your description when your delivery times change. "Same-day within
  campus" during a period when you cannot deliver same-day is the sort of small
  inaccuracy that turns into a dispute.
</Tip>

## Store password

Your store login is separate from your personal Soraxi account. Change the password from **Security → Change Password** in your store menu. You need your current password to set a new one.

<Warning>
  Change the temporary password from your approval email the first time you sign
  in, if you have not already. Emailed passwords are the weakest credential your
  store will ever have.
</Warning>

If you have forgotten it, use **Forgot Password** on the sign-in page with your **store email**. See [Resetting Your Password](/docs/account/password-reset).

Your store password is also what authorises publishing products — so it protects your catalogue, not just your login.

## Shipping methods

Open **Shipping** to add, edit, activate or deactivate delivery options. Each method has a name, a price, an estimated number of days, and a description.

Deactivating a method removes it from checkout without deleting it — useful when you cannot deliver a particular way for a while.

See [Setting Up Shipping](/docs/storefront/shipping-setup).

## Payout details

Open **Payment Setup** to manage the bank account your earnings are paid into. See [Payout Configuration](/docs/storefront/payout-settings).

<Note type="warning">
  Keep your payout account current. A withdrawal to a closed or wrong account
  fails, and sorting it out takes far longer than updating the details in advance.
</Note>

## Your store dashboard

The dashboard is the summary view: recent orders, what needs action, and how your store is doing. It is the right place to start each day — orders waiting to be processed are the most time-sensitive thing in your store.

## Messages

Open **Messages** to see buyer conversations, filtered by whether they are about a product or an order. Answering quickly is the cheapest thing you can do to protect your rating.

See [Handling Buyer Messages](/docs/fulfilment/order-fulfilment).

## What you cannot change yourself

- **Store name** — set from your application; contact [support](/support)
- **Store email** — it is your login; contact [support](/support)

## FAQs

**Q: Can I pause my store while I travel?**
A: There is no single holiday switch. Deactivate your shipping methods and hide your products, and tell buyers in your description when you will be back.

**Q: Can someone else help me run my store?**
A: There is one login per store. Anyone you share it with can do everything you can — including publishing products and requesting withdrawals. Share it carefully, and change the password afterwards.

**Q: I changed my description and nothing updated for buyers.**
A: Refresh the storefront page. If it persists, contact [support](/support).
